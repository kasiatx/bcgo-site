Where we hunt

A wilderness plateau northwest of Prince George.

Moose and bear hunts cover 900 square miles of timbered plateau laced with over 200 lakes and five major rivers. Mountain goat hunts run in the high country southwest of Smithers.

  • Lakes, mountains and river valleys — glassing country for days
  • Professionally built camps and guide cabins with wood heat
  • Remote tent camps for the far-flung hunts

Meet your outfitter

Mark Werner

Mark was raised by his dad to love hunting in British Columbia, and he's passed that same love on to his own family — who are living the dream of running a guide-outfitting business in the BC wilds.

He has served as President of the Guide Outfitters Association of BC, is past President of the BC Fishing Resorts & Outfitters Association, and started one of the largest wildlife DNA-collection programs in the province.

"The fun in camp, the friendly banter between guides, the practical jokes — most clients become good friends and stay in touch over the years."
