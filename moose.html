The hunt

Big bulls, big country.

Our moose hunts run northwest of Prince George across 900 square miles of timbered plateau, lakes and river valleys. Rifle dates are set for the peak of the rut, when bulls are responsive to calling and on the move.

Hunt one-on-one with your own guide, or share a guide with a partner on a 2×1 hunt. Northern Canadian moose are our bread and butter; Shiras moose hunts are also available — call Mark for details.

Any wounding of game is considered fatal — we'll spend the rest of the hunt looking until the outfitter is satisfied.
