Government fees

Licences, tags & royalties

Not included in hunt prices. Licence and tag fees are set by the Province of BC and include the HCTF surcharge (non-resident alien rates; Canadian non-residents pay less). Royalties are due at the end of the hunt. 2026 rates, subject to change — see gov.bc.ca.

Deposits & cancellations

All deposits are non-refundable. We recommend cancellation insurance — call Mark for cancellation details.

Wounded game

Any wounding of game is considered fatal, and the rest of the hunt will be spent looking for the animal until the outfitter is satisfied.

Bringing a firearm into Canada

Non-residents must complete the Non-Resident Firearm Declaration. See the Canadian government's firearm guidance for visitors.

Taxes & dates

GST of 5% applies to everything. Hunt prices do not include the hunting licence, species tags, Hunt Preservation Fee (HPF) or GST. Hunt dates are tentative and subject to change.
